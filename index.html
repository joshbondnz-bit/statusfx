Your night out, as an RPG

Status Effects is a drink tracker for iPhone. Log your drinks and watch your HP: every drink drains it based on your body, what you've eaten and how fast you're drinking. Debuffs like Impaired judgment, Dehydration and Hangover show what tonight is doing to you, while water, food and a slower pace earn buffs.

Private by design. No account, no ads, no tracking. Everything stays on your phone.

Coming soon to the App Store.

Status Effects gives estimates, not medical advice, and never tells you you're safe to drive. Never drink and drive. For people of legal drinking age.
