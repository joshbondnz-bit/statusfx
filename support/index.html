Support

Questions, bugs or ideas? Email connect.statusfx@outlook.com. Please include your phone model and what you were doing when the problem happened.

How accurate is my HP?

It's an estimate. The app models your blood alcohol from your weight, height, age, body-water formula, food and drinks, using standard formulas from the research (the Science tab lists every source). Real bodies vary, so treat the numbers as a guide, never as a breathalyser reading or a driving clearance.

Can it tell me when I'm safe to drive?

No, and it never will. Never drink and drive. Plan a taxi, rideshare, transit or a sober friend before your first drink.

Where is my data? Can I move it to a new phone?

Everything is stored only on your phone. If your phone backs up its apps (for example, iCloud Backup) and you restore that backup onto a new phone, your log comes with it.

How do I start over?

In the app, go to Options › Your data › Clear all history. Your character sheet, settings and saved drinks are kept.

The night didn't reset in the morning

A night moves to History once your usual wake time has passed and the alcohol has cleared. After a heavy night it stays on Tonight until the alcohol clears, so you can watch your HP recover. Check your wake time in Options.

In an emergency

If someone shows signs of alcohol poisoning (they can't be woken, have slow or irregular breathing, are vomiting while drowsy, or have cold, clammy or bluish skin), call your local emergency number straight away and stay with them.

Status Effects gives estimates, not medical advice. Never drink and drive.
